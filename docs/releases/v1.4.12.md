## 飞驴 v1.4.12 / Feilv v1.4.12

- 在线设备列表新增搜索框，可按设备名称或 IP 实时筛选，点击右侧叉号清空。
- 设备上下线、名称或地址变化时自动更新结果；无在线设备和筛选无结果显示不同提示。
- 筛选仅影响在线列表展示，发送接收人选择、拖拽发送和正在进行的传输保持原有行为。
- 搜索框和空状态支持中英文、深浅色主题。

---

- Added a live device filter by name or IP, with a clear button beside the search field.
- Results update as devices connect, disconnect, change names or change addresses. Empty search results and no online devices have separate messages.
- Filtering changes only the displayed list. Recipient selection, drag-and-drop and active transfers keep their existing behavior.
- The search and empty states support Chinese, English, dark and light themes.

更新与卸载保留本地数据。 / Updates and uninstallation preserve local data.
