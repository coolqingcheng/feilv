## 飞驴 v1.4.15 / Feilv v1.4.15

- 收到文件时，右下角接收窗口会主动请求激活并保持置顶，主窗口仍可留在托盘。
- 如果接收窗口被隐藏，后续文件到达时会重新显示；关闭后台接收通知后仍保持静默。
- 二维码上传完成后的提示也会请求激活并保持置顶。

---

- Incoming file prompts now request activation and stay on top while the main window remains in the tray.
- A new queued file brings a hidden receive prompt back. Disabling background receive notifications still keeps them quiet.
- Completed QR uploads also request activation and keep their notification on top.
