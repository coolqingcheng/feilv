## 飞驴 v1.4.17 / Feilv v1.4.17

- 发送文件或文件夹时，可以在对应的历史记录中查看实时速度。发送完成后，记录显示平均发送速度，重启后也能查看。
- 新版从传输时开始记录速度。旧记录没有速度数据。

---

- See the live send speed for each file or folder in History. After the transfer finishes, the row shows its average send speed, even after restarting Feilv.
- Speed is recorded for transfers made with this version. Older records have no speed data.
