## 飞驴 v1.4.10 / Feilv v1.4.10

- 手机或浏览器通过二维码上传文件后，桌面右下角显示接收完成提示，包含文件名、大小、来源 IP、100% 进度和接收速度，可直接打开所在文件夹并定位到文件，或关闭提示。
- 二维码上传纳入收发记录，支持文件名搜索、重新打开所在文件夹和删除记录；删除记录保留实际文件。
- 多文件上传的完成提示排队显示，正在进行的局域网接收保持原有流程。关闭后台接收提示时，上传和历史记录仍正常保存。
- 二维码按钮旁新增文件夹 SVG 按钮，一键打开普通接收目录 `Files`；上传完成提示按实际上传路径定位，支持自定义目录和同名文件重命名。
- 全程保持异步流式上传，完成通知复用连接并发限制，批量上传的提示队列使用有界内存。

---

- Browser and phone uploads now show a completed-receive popup in the desktop corner, with filename, size, source IP, full progress and average speed. Use Show in folder to locate the exact file, or Close to dismiss it.
- QR uploads now appear in transfer history, with filename search, folder actions and record deletion. Deleting a record keeps the uploaded file.
- Multiple completion notices are queued alongside LAN reception. Turning off background notifications still saves uploads and their history.
- Added a folder SVG shortcut beside QR sharing to open the ordinary `Files` reception directory. Upload completion actions locate the actual destination, including custom directories and renamed duplicate files.
- Uploads remain asynchronous and streamed, with bounded connections and completion notices.

更新与卸载保留本地数据。 / Updates and uninstallation preserve local data.
