## 飞驴 v1.4.11 / Feilv v1.4.11

- 移除客户端之间文件、文件夹和共享文件下载的 30 分钟总时长限制；连接建立仍有超时保护。
- 发送设备行、接收提示、接收记录和共享下载页面新增取消操作，取消后清理未完成文件并可重新传输。
- 发送准备阶段取消会停止当前批次中尚未发送的项目；仍按设备顺序发送，文件继续流式处理。
- 两端均需升级到 v1.4.11 或更新版本，才能去掉各自的旧版超时。取消后的重试从头开始，暂不支持断点续传。

---

- Removed the 30-minute total duration limit for client file, folder and shared-file transfers. Connection establishment remains time-limited.
- Cancel from the sending device row, receive prompt, receiving history or shared download page. Partial files are cleaned up and transfers can be retried.
- Cancelling during send preparation stops remaining items in the current batch. Device transfers remain sequential and streamed.
- Both clients need v1.4.11 or later to remove the old timeout at both ends. Retries start from the beginning; resume is not yet supported.

更新与卸载保留本地数据。 / Updates and uninstallation preserve local data.
